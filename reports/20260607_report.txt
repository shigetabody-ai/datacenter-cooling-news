1. LiquidStack Launches GigaModular CDU: Industry's First Scalable Direct-to-Chip Cooling Unit with 10MW Capacity
   （LiquidStack、業界初の拡張可能モジュール型CDU「GigaModular」を発表——最大10MW対応）
   Source: Data Center Dynamics
   Date: 2026-06-05
   URL: https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/liquidstack-launches-modular-cdu-able-to-offer-10mw-of-cooling-capacity/
   Summary: LiquidStack unveiled the GigaModular CDU at Datacloud Global Congress 2026 in France,
            claiming it is the industry's first scalable modular Coolant Distribution Unit supporting
            single-phase Direct-to-Chip heat loads from 2.5MW to 10MW. The system features a
            high-efficiency IE5 pump, dual brazed-plate heat exchangers, and front-only serviceability
            with no rear or end access required, enabling wall-adjacent installation. GigaModular
            offers 25% lower capex and floor space than traditional CDUs, and can operate in ambient
            temperatures up to 50°C, making it suitable for edge AI and hyperscale deployments.
   【日本語訳】LiquidStackがDatacloud Global Congress 2026（フランス）にてGigaModular CDUを発表。
   2.5MWから10MWまで段階拡張可能な業界初のモジュール型CDUで、単相DLC熱負荷に対応する。IE5
   高効率ポンプと二重プレート熱交換器を搭載し、フロントアクセスのみでサービス可能な設計で壁面
   設置にも対応。従来型CDU比でCAPEXと設置面積を25%削減し、最高50℃環境での稼働を実現する。

3. ZutaCore Raises $100M Series C to Scale Waterless Cooling for AI and HPC Data Centers
   （ZutaCore、AIおよびHPCデータセンター向け水不要液冷技術拡大へシリーズCで1億ドル超を調達）
   Source: HPCwire
   Date: 2026-06-02
   URL: https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/zutacore-raises-100m-series-c-to-scale-waterless-cooling-for-ai-and-hpc-data-centers/
   Summary: ZutaCore closed a $100M+ Series C funding round backed by Mitsubishi Electric, Carrier
            Ventures, and Samsung Ventures to scale its waterless direct-to-chip two-phase liquid

            in-package thermal management R&D for next-generation chip architectures.
