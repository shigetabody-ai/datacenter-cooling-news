   URL: https://news.asu.edu/20260518-environment-and-sustainability-turning-down-heat-data-centers
   Summary: Arizona State University researchers conducted the first field study to directly

            finding that downwind neighborhoods can be up to 4°F (2.2°C) warmer. Effects

            heat reuse systems—that minimize thermal waste dissipation into communities.

               影響範囲は約530mまで及ぶ。廃熱を地域に放散させない液冷・廃熱再利用
               技術の普及の必要性を科学的に裏付けた研究として注目される。
