   Summary: As AI workloads push rack power densities well past 25 kW and into
            triple digits for GPU clusters, survey data shows mounting pressure on
            operators to deploy direct liquid cooling or hybrid air-liquid setups.
            Liquid cooling capacity equaled air cooling capacity globally in 2025

            standard in new AI-ready builds.
