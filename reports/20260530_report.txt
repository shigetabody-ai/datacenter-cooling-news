   Source: New Atlas

   Summary: Scientists have developed 3D-printed pure copper cold plates using

            manufacturing. The plates deliver 32% better cooling than

            calculate that applying this technology across a 1 GW data center

            use by more than 90% on the cooling side.

               低減を実現。1GWのデータセンター全体に適用した場合、冷却電力消費を
               総消費電力の30%から1.1%にまで削減できると試算され、業界に大きな注目を
