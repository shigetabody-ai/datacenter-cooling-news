2. Telehouse Canada Undergoes Major Infrastructure Upgrade to Scale AI-Driven Organizations
   （TelehouseカナダがAI対応インフラへの大規模刷新を実施）
   Source: BusinessWire
   Date: 2026-05-12
   URL: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260512456773/en/Telehouse-Canada-Undergoes-Major-Infrastructure-Upgrade-to-Scale-AI-Driven-Organizations
   Summary: KDDI's Telehouse Canada deployed direct liquid-to-chip cooling technology at its
            Toronto data centers, becoming Canada's first liquid-cooled interconnection hub with
            support for rack densities up to 120kW per rack. The system removes up to 80% of heat

            power-intensive CRACs. Waste heat is captured and redirected to Enwave's closed-loop

              サーバー部品から発生する熱の最大80%を直接除去。廃熱はEnwaveの地域熱供給システム
              経由でトロント市の飲料水加熱に再利用される仕組みを構築した。
