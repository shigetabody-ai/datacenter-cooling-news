   Date: 2026-05-27
   URL: https://www.fujielectric.co.jp/about/news/detail/1205838_4830.html
   Summary: Fuji Electric launched the world's first ejector chiller for data centers in June 2026,
            capable of reducing server rack cooling electricity consumption by up to 85%. The unit
            operates without a compressor, using server waste heat as the energy source to drive a
            proprietary ejector that compresses and circulates refrigerant in a closed loop. Since

            conventional chillers with this technology can dramatically lower PUE and operational
            costs for water-cooled data center deployments.
