   Source: PR Newswire
   Date: 2026-06-03
   URL: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vertiv-expands-fluid-management-services-to-help-reduce-water-use-during-data-center-fluid-system-commissioning-302790111.html
   Summary: Vertiv introduced the Vertiv PurgeRite NearZero, a patent-pending fluid management
            service for commissioning closed-loop hydronic systems in North American data centers.

   最大91%削減、排水管理コスト最大34%削減を達成。ある拠点ではタンカー約300台分の輸送も削減した。
